**Nebula Drift** is my current main project — an arcade space game about drifting through
gravity wells with no brakes.

## The idea

You can't shoot. You can't stop. All you have is a thruster and the gravity of nearby
bodies. Chain slingshots together to build speed and outrun the collapse of the star system.
